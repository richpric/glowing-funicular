## Why LexHub?

Legal documents are living artifacts. They go through drafts, reviews, approvals, and revisions. Yet most legal teams still rely on email threads, shared drives, and file names like `contract_final_v3_ACTUAL_FINAL.docx`.

LexHub applies the proven workflows of software development—version control, pull requests, issue tracking, and code review—to legal work. This means:

- **Full audit trails** for every change, who made it, and when.
- **Branching and merging** for negotiating alternative clauses.
- **Review workflows** with approvals and comments.
- **Secure access controls** down to the paragraph level.

## Key Features

### 📄 Document Version Control
- Git-backed versioning for `.docx`, `.pdf`, `.md`, and plain text files.
- Visual diffs that highlight changes in legal language.
- Clause-level tracking with semantic diffing.

### 🔀 Branching & Merging
- Create branches for alternative negotiation positions.
- Merge approved changes into the main agreement.
- Resolve conflicts with a legal-aware merge tool.

### 👥 Review & Approval Workflows
- Pull requests for document changes.
- Required reviewers and approval gates.
- Inline comments and suggestions.

### 🔐 Granular Access Control
- Role-based permissions (Partner, Associate, Paralegal, Client, etc.).
- Matter-based access groups.
- Time-limited access for external counsel.

### 📊 Audit & Compliance
- Immutable audit logs.
- SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR compliant.
- Data residency options (US, EU, UK).
- Legal hold and retention policies.

### 🤖 AI-Assisted Review
- Automated clause extraction and classification.
- Risk flagging for non-standard terms.
- Citation checking and precedent lookup.

### 🔗 Integrations
- Microsoft Word & Outlook add-ins.
- DocuSign and Adobe Sign.
- iManage, NetDocuments, and SharePoint.
- Slack and Microsoft Teams notifications.
